# About

I am a physicist working on MRI methods and applications at Wellumio, where we are developing a portable brain scanner to detect the early signs of stroke. I completed my MSc and PhD in low-field MR physics at Victoria University of Wellington, in 2018 and 2022 here in Wellington, New Zealand. Now I'm enjoying start-up life!


## Interests
